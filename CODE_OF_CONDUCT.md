# Code of conduct

These projects are for everyone. We want taking part to be welcoming, safe and useful. This code is based on the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1, written here in plain English. It applies in
every cyberdudeuk repository and anywhere you represent one of its projects.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Welcome people of every background, identity, ability and level of experience.
- Write in plain English and explain jargon, so everyone can take part.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy: never share anyone's personal data, including your own.
- Say when you have a conflict of interest.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sharing official-sensitive, classified or confidential material.
- Spam, advertising, or using a project to sell.

## Reporting a problem

Use the [contact form](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/cyberdudeuk.github.io/issues/new?template=contact.yml): pick the
project and describe what happened. You don't need to give your name or email address. For
security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

The maintainer may remove content, ask someone to change their behaviour, or block someone from the project. We'll
explain the decision unless that would put someone at risk.
